Best practices for recording activities that require a large capture area, such as running, cutting, and triple hops.

:::tip
Before finalizing your setup, have the participant perform the activity while you monitor each camera's field of view, and adjust camera orientation to ensure the full movement is visible from all cameras.
:::

## 1. Recording Setup

Ensure cameras are correctly positioned to capture movement spanning a large area.

### Set up the cameras

- Mount the iOS devices on tripods in **landscape mode**.
  - *If using three devices, position the front-facing one in **portrait mode**.*
- Use the setup diagram below as a guide for tripod placement and angles.
- Open the Model Health app on each device and adjust positions until the **full capture area is visible** on every screen.

## 2. Setup calibration

- Place the checkerboard **perpendicular to the ground**, with its long edge parallel to the ground, visible to all devices, approximately **3–5 m from each device**.
- Once ready, press **"Calibrate"**.

Once calibration is complete, the checkerboard can be removed.

:::tip
If calibration fails, try moving the checkerboard slightly closer to the devices or adjusting the lighting conditions.
:::

## 3. Static pose

- Have the participant stand in a static pose near the checkerboard position: upright, feet parallel and pointing forward, arms slightly away from the body.
