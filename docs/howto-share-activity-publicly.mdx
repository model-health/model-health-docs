import React from 'react';

The subject's dashboard view lets you share one or more selected activities with a third party — useful for sharing results patients, athletes, or colleagues without access to your organization's account.

Sharing a dashboard publicly creates a unique, view-only public link that includes recorded videos, 3D model, raw kinematic data, and summary metrics. Only the selected activities are shared.

:::warning
Always obtain consent from participants before sharing their data with a third party.
:::

### Step 1. Select the activities you want to share from a subject's dashboard

In the **Activities** panel on the left, click any number of activities to load them into the metrics or Replay dashboard.

<ZoomableImage
  src="/img/docs/howto-share-data-publicly/howto-share-select-activities.png"
  alt="Select any number of completed activities to share"
  caption="Click any number of completed activities in the left panel to load them into the dashboard."
/>

### Step 2. Generate a sharing link to the loaded dashboard

Click the **Share** icon in the top-right corner to generate a public link to the loaded dashboard.

<ZoomableImage
  src="/img/docs/howto-share-data-publicly/howto-share-button.png"
  alt="Click the share icon in the top-right corner"
  caption="The Share icon generates a public, view-only link to the currently loaded dashboard."
/>

### Step 3. Copy the generated link

Copy the link from the dialog and share it with anyone — no account required to view.
