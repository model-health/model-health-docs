import React from 'react';

The subject's dashboard view lets you share one or multiple selected activities with third party — useful for sharing insights to non-users or colleagues without access to your organization's account.

Sharing a dashboard publicly will create a unique public link to your dashboard (View only): including recorded videos, 3D model, raw kinematics data and summary metrics.
Only selected activities are being shared. 

This is an example of publicly shared link: . 

:::warning
Make sure to always ask consent of the participants before sharing their data with a third party.
:::

### Step 1. Select the activities you want to share from a subject's dashboard

In the **Activities** panel on the left, click any number of activities to load them into the metrics or Replay dashboard.

<ZoomableImage
  src="/img/docs/howto-share-data-publicly/howto-share-select-activities.png"
  alt="Select any number of completed activities to share"
  caption="Click any number of completed activity in the left panel to load it into the dashboard."
/>

### Step 2. Generate a sharing link to the loaded dashboard

Click the Share icon on the top right corner of the screen to generate a public sharing link to the loaded dashboard.

<ZoomableImage
  src="/img/docs/howto-share-data-publicly/howto-share-button.png"
  alt="Click the share icon on the top right corner of the screen"
  caption="Share the loaded dashboard with an external user by clicking on the share icon on the top right of the screen"
/>
