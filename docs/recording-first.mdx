This guide walks you through your first complete 3D movement analysis, from calibrating your setup to exploring your results.

## Key Concepts

Before you start, familiarize yourself with the core building blocks of the app:

- **Subject**: The patient, athlete, or individual being assessed.
- **Group**: A collection of subjects used for comparative analysis or reporting.
- **Setup**: Your iOS devices mounted on tripods and calibrated. Once calibrated, a setup can be reused.
- **Activity type**: The category of movement being recorded (e.g., countermovement jump, hop test).
- **Activity**: A single recording with a subject, e.g., a running trial.
- **Metric**: A measured value from an activity, such as jump height or peak knee angle. Metrics vary by activity type.

## Step 1. Calibrate Your Set Up

Calibration tells the system exactly where your cameras are in space. You only need to do this once per setup. After that, you can record any number of activities without recalibrating, as long as the devices haven't moved.

### 1.1. Start a session

In the web app, click **Record activity**, then **Set up session**.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page7-img1.png" alt="Record activity button" />

### 1.2. Choose your set up configuration

Select the configuration that matches the activities you plan to record. Each option lists the activity types it supports.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page8-img1.png" alt="Choose setup configuration" />

### 1.3. Connect your devices

Install the Model Health app from the App Store on each iOS device. Open the app and scan the on-screen QR code to connect each device.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page9-img1.png" alt="Connect devices via QR code" />

### 1.4. Position your devices and the checkerboard

Follow the on-screen calibration guide to position your devices and the checkerboard:

- Mount all recording devices on tripods.
- Adjust the position of the devices until the entire capture area is visible on each device's screen.
- Place the checkerboard perpendicular to the ground, with its long edge parallel to the ground and visible to all devices, approximately 3-5 meters from each device.
- Watch this video for an example of setting up for overground gait recording — the same principles apply to all activities.

Once everything is in position, hit **Calibrate setup**. The process takes about 30 seconds.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page10-img1.jpeg" alt="Position devices and calibrate" />

### 1.5. Calibration complete

When calibration succeeds, hit **I'm ready to record**. Your calibrated setup remains active for 12 hours — do not move the devices during this time, as moving them invalidates the calibration and requires recalibrating before recording.

## Step 2. Record an Activity

Recording an activity follows a 4-step flow: Select subject → Record static pose → Select activity type → Record activity.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page12-img5.png" alt="Recording flow overview" />

### 2.1. Select or create a subject

Choose an existing subject from the dropdown or create a new one, then hit **Continue**.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page13-img1.png" alt="Select subject" />

### 2.2. Record a static pose

Ask the subject to stand still in a neutral position: upright, feet parallel and pointing forward, arms slightly away from the body. Make sure the full body is visible to all cameras. Hit **Record pose** — the recording lasts about 2 seconds, then takes around 30 seconds to process.

This lets the system adapt its 3D biomechanical model to the subject's proportions.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page14-img1.png" alt="Record static pose" />

Once the pose is recorded, hit **Continue**.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page15-img1.png" alt="Static pose recorded" />

### 2.3. Select the activity type

Select the movement type the subject will perform and hit **Confirm**.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page16-img1.png" alt="Select activity type" />

### 2.4. Start recording

Review the tips for best data quality, then hit **Start recording** when the subject is ready:

- Ensure the subject is fully visible to at least 2 cameras.
- Tight clothing that contrasts with the background improves tracking.
- Hit record 1 second before the subject begins the movement.

You can optionally add **tags** and **notes** before, during, or after the recording.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page17-img1.png" alt="Ready to record" />

### 2.5. Stop recording

Hit **Stop recording** as soon as the activity is complete. The shorter the recording, the faster the processing.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page19-img1.png" alt="Stop recording" />

Processing starts automatically in the background. You can continue recording other activities or go directly to results.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page20-img1.png" alt="Processing started" />

## Step 3. Explore Results

### 3.1. View videos and 3D model

From the subject page, select the activity type, then select one or more recordings to review the synchronized videos, 3D motion reconstruction, and kinematic data.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page22-img1.jpeg" alt="View results - replay" />

### 3.2. Visualize metrics

Switch to the **Metrics** tab to see key metrics. Select multiple recordings for comparison.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page23-img1.png" alt="View results - metrics" />

### 3.3. Download reports

Use the download button (top right) to export single-activity and comparison reports, analysis data, or raw data.

<ZoomableImage src="/img/docs/recording-first/page24-img1.png" alt="Download PDF report" />
